Modal Logic as Metaphysics

Modal Logic as Metaphysics (2013). Oxford: Oxford University Press.

Google Scholar: 1,416 citations As of 20 August 2026.

Timothy Williamson gives an original and provocative treatment of deep metaphysical questions about existence, contingency, and change, using the latest resources of quantified modal logic.
